# Where the side panel opens

By default the usermods side panel belongs to the tab you opened it on: switch tabs and it is gone, come back and it is there on the same chat. To have one panel that follows you across tabs, set **Side panel opens** to **On every tab** in Settings.

## The two settings

| Side panel opens | What happens |
|---|---|
| On this tab only (default) | The panel stays with its tab. It always shows that site's chats and mods. |
| On every tab | Chrome's window-wide panel, which follows you from tab to tab until you close it. |

The change applies the next time you click the toolbar icon. No reload is needed.

## Opening and closing

Click the usermods icon in the toolbar to open the panel. Chrome gives extensions no way to close a side panel from the icon, so close it with the close button in the panel's own header.

## Getting back to a chat

Chats are kept per site. To reopen a chat from another site, use the [dashboard](/guide/dashboard): **Open** loads the page the chat was last used on with the panel showing that chat.
